Taste in motion

Notes from travel: places, meals, routes, and the small details that stick around.

Travel makes comparison hard to avoid. Cities, materials, food, routes, and habits show you what you notice once daily life changes. Small choices that blend into a routine at home become obvious somewhere unfamiliar.

That change of context is often more useful than a checklist of highlights. A market, a train platform, or a long walk across a city can show you how people use space, spend time, and make something ordinary feel considered.

The details that stay with me are usually small: a material choice, a meal ordered twice, a route that becomes familiar, or a public space that works because it gives people room to use it their own way.

Travel does not always provide an answer. It provides better comparisons, and a few new ways to notice what was already there.
