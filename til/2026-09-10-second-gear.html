Pre-read: Enterprise AI cost control is moving from procurement theater into runtime systems: Databricks describes model choice, task routing, spend gates, token-overhead reduction, and AI gateways as the practical levers large engineering orgs are standardizing. The piece is worth opening because it gives company-level texture to that operating shift.

Summary: Gergely Orosz reports that Uber, Pinterest, AT&T, Stripe, Coinbase, and Ramp are cutting AI spend by shifting work toward open models, smarter routing, benchmarking, prompt caching, context compaction, and effort controls. Uber reportedly cut cost per AI request by 34% and cost per AI session by 52%, while Pinterest said open models cost less than 8% of comparable closed-model transactions for some use cases. The useful founder angle is that AI spend governance is becoming a control plane across harnesses, models, context, and tool calls, not a dashboard bolted onto token bills.
